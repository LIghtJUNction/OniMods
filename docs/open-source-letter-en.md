# A Letter to Everyone Who Finds This Project Interesting

To everyone who finds this project interesting:

Recently, I have been working on something a little unusual.

It is called OniMods.

One of the projects inside it is an MCP Mod that allows AI to enter the world of *Oxygen Not Included*.

The idea is not simply to let AI answer questions.

I wanted to explore something deeper:

Can AI understand a world with rules, observe its environment, make decisions, and actually become a participant rather than just a tool?

That curiosity is what started this project.

But during development, I quickly discovered a problem:

This is expensive.

Every decision AI makes consumes Tokens.

And in a continuously running environment like a game, the cost can grow very quickly.

I am just a university student.

I do not have a company, investors, or a development team behind me.

Most of the cost has been supported by myself.

I have spent countless hours writing code, testing models, debugging problems, and using a large amount of Tokens just to make this idea work.

Sometimes I ask myself:

Is a personal project really worth this much effort?

But every time I see AI starting to understand the game world, every time someone becomes interested in this experiment, I feel that it is worth continuing.

I do not want this to become a simple donation request.

Instead, I hope to create a way where people can support the project while also receiving something valuable.

So I created:

[https://api.lmm.best](https://api.lmm.best)

It is an AI API service platform.

You can purchase Tokens here to use AI services, and at the same time help me cover the ongoing development costs of OniMods.

A part of the revenue will be used to support open-source development. The source code is open.

I also want to give something back to people who contribute.

If you are willing to:

* Submit Issues and help find problems;
* Submit Pull Requests and improve the code;
* Share ideas and suggestions;
* Help test new features;

I will provide Token rewards whenever possible.

I hope to build a small cycle:

People use the service and help the project continue.

People contribute to open source and make it better.

I keep investing my time to turn more interesting ideas into reality.

OniMods is still a small project today.

But many meaningful things start from a simple thought:

“What if this could actually work?”

And then someone spends the time to make it real.

Thank you to everyone who follows, uses, contributes to, or supports this project.

I hope this curiosity can continue for a long time.
