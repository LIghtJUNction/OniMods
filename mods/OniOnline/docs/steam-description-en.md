[h1]OniOnline[/h1]

[b]Multiplayer Framework for Oxygen Not Included (Preview)[/b]

Never survive alone! Gather your friends in a shared lobby, chat in real-time inside your colony, and open the door to true multiplayer in Oxygen Not Included.

[h2]🌟 Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Instant Matchmaking & Lobbies[/b]: Full support for Steam invites, local LAN discovery, and 6-character room codes. Create or join rooms in seconds.
[*] [b]In-Game Seamless Chat[/b]: Press [b]Ctrl+T[/b] or click the management bar bubble inside your colony anytime to pop up the chat window and talk tactics as you build.
[*] [b]Smart Mod Alignment[/b]: Automatically verifies that all players have identical mod lists and versions before launching, preventing desyncs.
[*] [b]Extensible Gameplay SDK[/b]: Built as a robust multiplayer engine. Independent mods (such as One-Planet-Per-Player or inter-planetary trade) can plug right into its shared sessions, lobbies, and synchronized state channels.
[*] [b]Seamless Reconnect[/b]: Automatic session recovery and state handshakes if your network hiccups.
[/list]

[h2]🎮 Quick Start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] [b]Browse / Host[/b]: Click [b]“Browse Lobbies”[/b] on the main menu to search for public games or host your own room and share the code.
[*] [b]Chat In-Game[/b]: Once loaded into a colony, hit [b]Ctrl+T[/b] to communicate with fellow asteroid survivors.
[*] [b]Expand Gameplay[/b]: Install companion mods that declare OniOnline compatibility to unlock planetary registration, shared star maps, and specialized multiplayer rulesets.
[/olist]

[h2]💡 Status & Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Current Stage[/b]: This is a [b]v0.5.0 Development Preview[/b]. It focuses on solid lobby management, communication, and SDK extensibility. Cross-world remote construction and rocket visits are actively in development.
[*] [b]Save Safety[/b]: We recommend using fresh or backup saves while participating in the preview.
[*] [b]Compatibility[/b]: Supports both the Base Game and the Spaced Out! DLC.
[/list]
