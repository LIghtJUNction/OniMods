[h1]OniOnline[/h1]

[b]Multiplayer Framework for Oxygen Not Included (Preview)[/b]

Join a shared lobby, chat in real-time inside your colony, and build upon a modular multiplayer architecture for Oxygen Not Included.

[h2]Core Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Lobbies & Matchmaking[/b]: Steam invites, LAN discovery, and 6-character room codes for quick room creation and joining.
[*] [b]In-Game Communications[/b]: Press Ctrl+T or click the management bar bubble to communicate while building your base.
[*] [b]Mod Verification[/b]: Automatically compares required mods and versions across players before launch to prevent desyncs.
[*] [b]Extensible SDK[/b]: Built as a dedicated multiplayer engine. Gameplay mods (such as One-Planet-Per-Player or interstellar logistics) plug directly into shared sessions and synchronized state channels.
[*] [b]Connection Recovery[/b]: Automatically retains session context and attempts recovery during transient network drops.
[/list]

[h2]Getting Started[/h2]
[olist]
[*] [b]Host or Join[/b]: Click "Browse Lobbies" on the main menu to join public sessions or host a room.
[*] [b]In-Game Chat[/b]: Press Ctrl+T once your colony loads to access communications.
[*] [b]Gameplay Extensions[/b]: Pair with OniOnline-compatible gameplay mods to unlock planetary registration and shared star maps.
[/olist]

[h2]Development Status[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Current Scope[/b]: This is a v0.5.0 preview focused on lobby stability, in-game messaging, and SDK architecture. Cross-colony construction and direct rocket visitation are under active development.
[*] [b]Save Safety[/b]: Please use test saves or back up important colonies during preview testing.
[*] [b]Compatibility[/b]: Supports both the Base Game and Spaced Out! DLC.
[/list]
